My story

My name is Lynnet Njangiru and I live in Nairobi. I joined the IYF Weekend Academy because I want to understand how websites are made instead of only using them.

Before this course I had never written code. My first page was just a heading and one paragraph, but I was very happy when it opened in the browser.

Week 2 changed the way I look at websites. Now I notice spacing, alignment and font sizes everywhere I go. I still have a lot to learn, but I am no longer afraid of CSS.
